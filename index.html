Problem

Small Indonesian online sellers need to post consistently and know whether they are making a profit, but most have no time or tools for content planning and bookkeeping.

What I built

A SaaS that combines content planning, AI-generated content, and sales/profit tracking. It is a vanilla JavaScript PWA (56 modules, about 18k lines) on Supabase, with about 240 commits.

Technical highlights

  • Studio: users describe their business and the AI generates batches of post ideas (title, caption, hashtags). These are rendered into on-brand images in the browser with Canvas. Users swipe Tinder-style to approve, and approved posts go straight into a content calendar.
  • Server-side LLM proxy (Gemini, with a Claude path), so users need no API keys. It has per-user and per-IP daily quotas, a model fallback chain, and output validation that rejects malformed JSON or stray foreign-script tokens and retries.
  • Integrations: OAuth for Instagram (Facebook Login for Business), Threads, and TikTok. Access tokens are encrypted at rest (AES-GCM) and never sent to the browser. Also a comment inbox with AI-suggested replies, analytics from platform APIs, and Midtrans payments for subscriptions.
  • Backend: Postgres with Row Level Security, Google Auth, Storage, and 21 Supabase Edge Functions (Deno/TypeScript).
  • Delivery: Cloudflare Workers hosting with auto-deploy on push, plus a Capacitor Android build.
  • Growth: an onboarding funnel (/mulai) that generates a user's first 3 posts before signup, free SEO tools (caption generator, Instagram bio generator, shopping-holiday calendar, profit calculator) that feed the funnel, and first-party visit/funnel tracking.

SCNTR marketing automation

Built for my own perfume brand · runs on one Mac

Problem

Running a small brand's daily marketing by hand does not scale for one person: uploads, replies, giveaways, stock checks and reports all repeat every day.

What I built

About 150 Node.js scripts (about 21.6k lines) and 46 scheduled jobs under macOS launchd that handle most of that routine.

Technical highlights

  • Scheduled TikTok and Instagram uploads through a headless Chrome controlled over the Chrome DevTools Protocol, with per-item restarts and post-upload verification.
  • Threads posting and reply assistance.
  • A daily giveaway pipeline: video generated from text, scheduling, and random winner selection.
  • Stock and order monitoring, with daily and weekly reports sent to Telegram.
  • A watchdog job that checks the other jobs and reports failures.
  • Orchestration of several AI coding agents: a task queue and manager script assign work to CLI agents under written rules and guardrails, and I review their output.
